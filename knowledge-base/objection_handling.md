## 0.  The Core Belief

> *“Objections are just ****unanswered questions****.”*
>
> Our job is to surface the real question, not to bulldoze the prospect.

---

## 1.  Four Levels of the Problem

| Level            | Prospect’s focus                        | Your goal              |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1 — **External** | Tools, tactics, products.               | Drill deeper.          |
| 2 — **Process**  | Steps/sequence they’ve tried.           | Find gaps.             |
| 3 — **Internal** | Self‑beliefs, skills, bandwidth.        | Illuminate doubt.      |
| 4 — **Identity** | “I’m just not the kind of person who …” | Re‑author their story. |

We won’t win an identity‑level objection with feature talk.  Match the level → then lead them up.

---

## 2.  The 5‑Step Handling Flow

```text
a. Cushion     →  “Totally hear you…”
b. Clarify     →  “Just so I’m 100 % clear, when you say ___ what do you mean?”
c. Isolate     →  “Aside from ___, is there anything else holding you back?”
d. Re‑frame    →  *Bridge* from their answer ➜ the value you just covered.
e. Close       →  Confirm next step / call‑to‑action.
```

### Example – *“Need to Talk to My Partner”*

| Stage    | Mason’s line                                                                                                                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cushion  | “I respect that – big decisions deserve alignment.”                                                                                     |
| Clarify  | “Out of curiosity, what input do you expect from them?”                                                                                 |
| Isolate  | “If they give you the thumbs‑up, are you personally a *yes*?”                                                                           |
| Re‑frame | “Sounds like they care about ROI as much as you.  The fastest way to show them ROI is getting started while the promo pricing is live…” |
| Close    | “Want to loop them in right now, or go ahead and secure your spot and surprise them with progress?”                                     |

---

## 3.  Rapid‑Fire Cushions

- “Fair question…“
- “Makes sense you’d ask that…“
- “Others felt that way at first…“

Mix & match – keep it human.

---

## 4.  Objection → Root Question Cheat‑Sheet

| Objection heard  | Likely root question                             | Level    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| “Too expensive.” | *“Will this pay off for ****me****?”*            | Internal |
| “Need to think.” | *“Do I trust ****myself**** to follow through?”* | Identity |
| “Send info.”     | *“Is it safe to disengage right now?”*           | Process  |
| “I’ll DIY it.”   | *“What’s the worst that happens if I stay put?”* | Cost     |
