## title: "The Script‑less Sales Call System" author: Mason Church tags: [sales, discovery, pitch]

> **Purpose** – Enable ChatGPT (or new reps) to recreate Mason’s full 45‑min call flow *without memorising a rigid script*.

---

## 1.  Buying Beliefs (prerequisites to the sale)

1. **Pain** – Prospect admits a problem.
2. **Doubt** – DIY no longer sounds smart.
3. **Cost** – Inaction now feels *expensive*.
4. **Desire** – Bright emotional vision.
5. **Responsibility** – Ownership shifts inward.

## 2.  Eight‑Section Discovery Flow

| # | Section     | Key Questions                        |
| - | ----------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1 | Rapport     | *Highlights of your day?* (≤2 min)   |
| 2 | Frame       | “Here’s how these 45 min will roll…” |
| 3 | North‑Star  | “Outcome you want in 3‑6 mo?”        |
| 4 | Situation   | Numbers, behaviours, current plan    |
| 5 | Problem     | “Do you *like* those results?”       |
| 6 | Doubt       | “Why not keep doing it that way?”    |
| 7 | Cost/Desire | Emotional high & low                 |
| 8 | Commitment  | “Nice‑to‑have or must?”              |

## 3.  Pitch Template (P‑P‑S)

```txt
PROMISE  – Name, outcome, timeline.
PROCESS  – 3‑4 pillars, each followed by ► so‑that‑you‑can + which‑means‑to‑you.
STRUCTURE – Coaching / Course / Community (or equivalent delivery).
```

## 4.  Final Tie‑Down & Price Ask

1. Certainty check  →  “Do you feel this can get you to X?”
2. *Why*  →  Make them say it.
3. Funding statement  →  Options.
4. Next‑step  →  “What would you like to do next?”

## 5.  Negative‑question arsenal

- “Why not keep doing it the way you are now?”
- “Is this a ‘nice‑to‑have’ or a ‘must’?”
